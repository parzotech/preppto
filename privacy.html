We collect and process:

We use this data to:

We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with trusted service providers that help us run the app, under appropriate confidentiality and contractual terms. These include:

We may also share data if required by law.

4. Security and retention

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data. We retain data as long as needed to provide the service and as required by law.
